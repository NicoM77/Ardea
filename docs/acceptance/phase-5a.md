# Phase 5a acceptance: stack, notices and reminders

Master plan chapter 14, phase 5a, and the task-level promises of chapter 17 (17.1 to 17.7). Criteria are checked by
fast tests in the pipeline, by the opt-in spike `NoticeSpike`, or observed once on a real instance. This record is
written from the tests of `main` at the merge of PR #96 (tasks 5a.1 to 5a.6, PRs #84, #89, #92, #93, #94, #95 and
#96); nothing was observed on an instance. Every test runs with test data and, where time matters, a fake clock; the
spike week is the one real data set, and only the opt-in spike reads all of it.

The tests of the phase live in `Ardea.Assessment.Tests` (notices, spike), `Ardea.Iam.Tests` (the stack API),
`Ardea.Assets.Tests` (version precision), `Ardea.Notifications.Tests` (`notice.raised`, reminders by level),
`Ardea.Host.Tests` (OpenAPI document, migration) and the Playwright flows in `web/e2e/flows.spec.ts`.

## The acceptance of the phase

The master plan names three criteria: «A stack of ten entries produces at most the number of notices set in 5a.1 in
the spike week (N = 25, ADR 044); "doesn't affect us" does not come back; no endpoint and no page knows assignment
or deadline.»

## Open points for the owner

- **Confirm the reminder rhythm (E26).** Master plan 17.5 and ADR 048 propose level 5 daily, level 4 weekly and
  level 3 monthly, lower levels only in the digests, always on weekdays at 08:00 in the organisation's time zone.
  It is implemented as the default and is set per organisation on the rules page. The owner has not confirmed
  the rhythm or the run time; chapter 15 still lists it as a recommendation.
- **Sign off the master plan changes of PR #96:** chapter 1 (the MVP table: «escalation in steps» removed, escalation
  chains with deadlines moved to «dropped altogether»), chapter 8 (title «Benachrichtigungen und Erinnerungen», 8.2 and
  8.7 rewritten as reminders by level), chapter 9.2 (system operations), chapter 14 (later stages, item 4) and chapter
  15 (E26's note).
- **«Affects us» with a version but without a project makes the stack entry exact** (17.1): the stack is the place
  then, and the version is recorded there. For a notice that names components in several projects the answer is
  refused until a project is named, because a version belongs to one place (ADR 047 decision 7). The owner should
  confirm that this is what 17.3 meant.
- **A stack entry added later brings 80 to 500 «Earlier» notices** from the 180 days of look-back, if every week
  looked like the spike week (ADR 047). They are quiet (no `notice.raised`, one summary message) and are not counted
  as new, but the owner may want a shorter look-back for the stack or a way to dismiss them at once.
- **The mockup questions** (review of `notices.html` and `stack.html` against the built pages; deviations in PRs #92
  and #94): the keys 1 and 2 answer instead of the mockup's A and D; «Mark as remediated» needs no reason and has
  Undo, other outcomes need one; the three answers are on every reminder card; there is no rhythm chart on the
  overview and no «Reminds / Next reminder» in the reminder details; whether an entry in the stack is a line or exact
  follows what is typed (with a «Line / Exact» select), not a per-product depth of the catalogue, so exact entries
  are possible in the stack; the answered view is not limited to «this week».
- **The mockups are only at the tag `archive/planning-and-design`**: [`notices.html`](https://github.com/NicoM77/Ardea/tree/archive/planning-and-design/docs/design/mockups/notices.html)
  and [`stack.html`](https://github.com/NicoM77/Ardea/tree/archive/planning-and-design/docs/design/mockups/stack.html),
  not on `main`.
- **Nothing was observed on a real instance.** The criteria are checked with test data and a fake clock; the one
  real data set is the spike week of 11 to 17 November 2026, from the CVE Program only (no OSV or GitHub advisories,
  no NCSC warnings, no KEV or EPSS feed: ADR 044 limitations). Another week will differ. The stacks of the spike are
  built from master plan 17.8, not taken from organisations. How reminders and notices behave over weeks on a real
  instance belongs to the acceptance of all phases on the release images (task 7.11).
- **The release gate is over.** ADR 046 decision 13 and the README asked for no release or upgrade between 5a.2 and
  5a.5; 5a.5 is merged (PR #94), so the gate has fallen.
