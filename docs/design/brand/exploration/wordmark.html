Ardea wordmark

Heron B with three typefaces. Each face gets its own heron line weight so mark and letters carry the same stroke. All three fonts are open source (SIL Open Font License) and can be self-hosted.

1

Inter Tight 600 · heron line 3.8

Neutral grotesque, close to the system font of the interface. Calm and technical; the heron gets a medium line.

2

Source Serif 4 500 · heron line 2.3

Display serif with fine contrast. Reads like a bulletin or an institute; the heron is drawn in a thin, precise line.

3

Manrope 800 · heron line 5

Geometric sans in a heavy cut. Sturdy and confident at small sizes; the heron gets a bold line to match.
