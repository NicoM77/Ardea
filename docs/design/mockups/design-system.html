Design system

Tokens and components of the mockups. The source of truth is app.css.

Principles

  • Familiar before clever. Standard controls in their platform form: segmented controls, pop-up buttons, inset lists, a sidebar on desktop and a tab bar on phones.

  • One next step per screen. One blue primary button per view. Everything else is secondary or plain.

  • Colour means priority. Apart from the blue for actions and links, colour appears only on a priority, and always with its symbol and its word.

  • Calm by default. Grey canvas, white groups, hairlines. No shadows except the segmented thumb, no motion except state changes of 150 ms.

Priority

High raised to Critical

Each priority has its own shape: octagon, triangle, diamond, circle with arrow, circle with dash. Shape and word carry the meaning; colour repeats it. The word is always visible.
