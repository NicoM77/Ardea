What raises a notice

Only important
Critical (CVSS 9.0 or more), actively exploited, warned about by a national authority, or likely to be exploited soon. Browsers only when exploited or warned about. The default.
Everything
Every entry that names the product, within the version line.
The product itself
An entry covers the product, not what is built on it: .NET says nothing about NuGet packages, WordPress nothing about plugins. Connect a repository to a project for those.

Mockup with sample data. “Last week” is the spike week of ADR 044 (11–17 Nov 2026) for these entries: 1,141 entries named the products, 19 met the threshold. A version line such as “17” narrows notices to that line; an exact version such as “17.9” leaves the stack and is checked like a project, with findings and reminders (master plan 17.1). Every change is saved at once and confirms with Undo. The organization is invented.
